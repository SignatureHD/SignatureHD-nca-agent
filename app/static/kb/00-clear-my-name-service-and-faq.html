ABOUT CLEAR MY NAME

What is Clear My Name?

Clear My Name (clearmy.name) helps South Africans understand their credit record and take the right next step. We read your credit report, show you what is wrong, what is accurate, and which steps to take first. Much of what people need to do is free, and we show them how to do it.

Who is it for?

South Africans who have been declined for credit (a loan, vehicle finance, a home loan, a store card or a contract), who have been told they are "blacklisted", who see something on their credit report they do not recognise, or who want to check their record before applying for credit. We also help small business owners check their company credit.

Where are you based?

We work online and help people anywhere in South Africa. There is no walk-in office.

THE FREE FINDINGS AND ACTION PLAN

What is the Findings and Action Plan?

A free, personal report based on your credit report. It shows why you may have been declined, which entries look wrong and can be disputed for free, which entries are accurate and will stay, and the steps to take first. A professional checks every plan before it reaches you.

Is it really free?

Yes. The Findings and Action Plan costs nothing and there is no obligation. You never pay upfront.

How do I get my free plan?

Use the secure form at https://link.signatureai.co.za/widget/form/F6ci7oNSU1bTQ7gThuCF (the "Get my free plan" button on the website). You can either:

1. Upload your credit report (a PDF from TransUnion, Experian, XDS or VeriCred), or

2. Ask us to get your credit report for you. You give your consent on the form, and we request the report from a credit bureau on your behalf.

After you submit the form we email you to confirm what happens next.

How long does it take?

We start working on your report as soon as it arrives. A professional checks the plan before it is sent, and we email it to you as soon as it is ready. You do not need to do anything in the meantime.

What happens after I get my plan?

You can follow the steps yourself. If you would like to talk it through, you can book a free review call at https://link.signatureai.co.za/widget/bookings/clearmyname-free-review, or reply to the plan email.

Can I send my credit report in this chat, by email or by WhatsApp?

Please do not. A credit report contains your ID number and account details. Upload it only through the secure form at https://link.signatureai.co.za/widget/form/F6ci7oNSU1bTQ7gThuCF so it goes straight to the right place.

Why do you need my consent to get my credit report?

The law only allows a credit report to be requested for a proper purpose, and a third party needs your consent to request yours. The consent box on the form gives us that permission for this one purpose: preparing your Findings and Action Plan. We use your information only for that and handle it under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

Does checking my credit report affect my credit score?

No. Checking your own credit report, or us checking it with your consent to help you, does not lower your score.

COSTS

What does it cost?

The checker, the free guides and the Findings and Action Plan are free. Getting your own credit report from a bureau is free once every 12 months from each bureau. Disputing wrong information with a credit bureau is free. Complaining to the National Financial Ombud Scheme or the National Credit Regulator is free.

When would I pay anything?

Only for help with complex matters, for example legal work to rescind a judgment, or debt counselling if you cannot afford your repayments. Any fee is explained and agreed with you first, and you never pay upfront. Debt counselling fees are set by regulation.

Should I pay a company to clean my credit record?

Be careful. Nobody can lawfully remove accurate information before its time. A credit repair provider may not make you pay before the work is fully done. Ask for the scope in writing and never pay to have accurate information removed.

CREDIT REPORT BASICS

Is there a blacklist in South Africa?

No. There is no single blacklist. Credit bureaux keep a record of how you have paid your accounts, any court judgments and certain other events. Lenders read that record and decide for themselves.

Which credit bureaux are there?

The main consumer credit bureaux are TransUnion (mytransunion.co.za), Experian (experian.co.za), XDS (xds.co.za) and VeriCred (vericred.co.za). Each may hold slightly different information, which is one reason scores differ.

How do I get my free credit report?

You are entitled to one free credit report from each credit bureau every 12 months. Request it on the bureau's website or app. You can then upload it to us for your free plan.

Why was I declined?

The lender must tell you the main reason if you ask, and if it was because of your credit report, which credit bureau it used (National Credit Act, section 62). Ask the lender in writing, then check that bureau's report.

How long does negative information stay on my record?

It depends on the type. Under the National Credit Regulations, for example: enquiries stay for 1 year, adverse classifications (such as "default" or "handed over") for 1 year, payment profile information for 5 years, civil court judgments for 5 years or until rescinded or abandoned if earlier, administration orders for 5 years or until rescinded, and a debt review listing until a clearance certificate is issued. Paid-up judgments must be removed once settled (National Credit Act, section 71A). The exact rule depends on the entry, so the Findings and Action Plan checks each one.

What can be disputed?

Information that is wrong: an account that is not yours, a payment marked missed when it was paid, a duplicate entry, a listing kept longer than allowed, information not updated, a listing made without the required notice, or a debt that has prescribed. The credit provider must produce credible evidence when information is disputed; if it cannot, the bureau must correct or remove it.

What is prescribed debt?

In general, an ordinary unsecured debt prescribes (falls away) after 3 years if you have not paid anything towards it, have not acknowledged it in writing, and no summons was served on you. A creditor may not sell or try to collect a debt that has prescribed (National Credit Act, section 126B). Paying even a small amount on an old debt can restart the clock, so get advice first. We check dates on your report in the Findings and Action Plan.

What is debt review?

Debt review (debt counselling) is a legal process for people who cannot afford their monthly repayments. A registered debt counsellor restructures the repayments. While under debt review you cannot take on new credit. When all debts covered (other than a home loan) are paid, a clearance certificate is issued and the listing is removed. Clear My Name is not the same as debt review; it focuses on your credit record. If repayments are a struggle, we will talk you through your options.

What is reckless credit?

Credit given without a proper affordability assessment, or when the lender should have seen that you could not afford it. A court may set aside or suspend a reckless credit agreement. If your report shows signs of this, your Findings and Action Plan will say so.

What about company credit?

Business owners can check their company's credit profile before applying for funding or tenders. Ask us and we will point you to the right next step.
